About Just Weather

An independent weather dashboard using Open-Meteo, RainViewer, WXIndex and Radio Browser data services. Weather and radio availability can vary by provider. This is a web-app edition and does not yet include every feature of the Roku app.

Tip: in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to launch it like an app.
