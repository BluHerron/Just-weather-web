About Just Weather

An independent weather dashboard using Open-Meteo weather data, RainViewer radar, WXIndex station data, Radio Browser station listings, and National Weather Service alerts. Radar map display provided by WeatherScope. Weather and radio availability can vary by provider. This is a web-app edition and does not yet include every feature of the Roku app.

Tip: in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen to launch it like an app.
